Students now judge an institution by the same standard they apply to every app, store, banking platform and streaming service they use, and the higher education digital experience is where that judgment begins. Personalized web experiences move prospective students toward enrollment. When a site adapts to who a visitor is and what they came for, learners keep going instead of returning to the search bar.
