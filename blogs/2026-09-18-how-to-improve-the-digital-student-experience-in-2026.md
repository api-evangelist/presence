The digital student experience is now the front door to an institution, and it shapes whether learners enroll, stay and return. Nearly all learners arrive on a phone, so mobile-first design is the baseline for a strong digital experience, not an upgrade. AI-powered personalization turns generic web pages into relevant, learner-specific paths that lift inquiry conversions.
