Ask alumni what they remember most about college, and they'll often tell stories unrelated to the classroom. They might talk about the first Welcome Week event where they met lifelong friends , the first Homecoming they attended, the rivalry game they camped out for, or the candlelight ceremony before graduation. Those moments often become the defining memories of campus life.
