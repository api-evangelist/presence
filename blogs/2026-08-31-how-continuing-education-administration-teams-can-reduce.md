Continuing education administration gets complicated because of program variety, not program volume, which means another hire won't solve a structural problem. The top barriers to expanding credentials are now administrative burden and the pressure to respond quickly to market demand, overtaking the faculty resistance that dominated this conversation for years. Only 27% of continuing education units say their technology integrates cleanly with main campus systems, which turns staff into the integration layer between disconnected tools.
